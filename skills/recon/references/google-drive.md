# Google Drive recon

Use the Google Drive MCP. Search for the Gemini-generated notes of Google Meet meetings that happened close to the time the task was created — they often carry annotations and transcriptions of the team talking about something related to the task.

If the Google Drive MCP is not available, this is the one sanctioned fallback in this recon: use the `gws-work` CLI tool instead. (This is not routing around a broken MCP — it is the tool to use when the Drive MCP is not connected at all.)
