# Google Calendar

Use the Google Calendar MCP on the primary calendar.

This surface answers the time half of a question. "What should I pick up now" depends as much on having a free afternoon as on what is in the backlog.

## Sweeping for an answer

- **The free blocks in the window.** `list_events` over today and the rest of the week, then report the uninterrupted gaps and how long each is. Two hours before the next meeting rules out anything that needs a day.
- **What is coming that changes the answer.** A planning or review meeting later today can make a task urgent, or make it pointless to start.
- **Who you are meeting.** When a name surfaced on another surface also appears on the calendar, say so — a question waiting on someone you meet in two hours has a cheaper answer than a search.

## Reporting

Free blocks as date, start, end, duration. Relevant events as date, time, title, attendees. Keep it to what bears on the question; a full agenda dump is not evidence.
